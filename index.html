Explain the Difference: Aadhaar Linked vs. DBT Enabled

Don't just read a paragraph. Explore our visual comparison matrix and interactive flowchart to understand how scholarship money moves.

📋 Visual Comparison Matrix

Key Concepts
Comparison Point Aadhaar Linked (KYC)
Identity Association Only 		DBT Enabled (NPCI Seeded)

📋 Core Meaning Aadhaar Linked (KYC)
Aadhaar is associated with your bank account as an identity / KYC document. 		DBT Enabled (NPCI Seeded)
Account is enabled and mapped in the central NPCI APBS to receive DBT payments directly.
🎯 Primary Purpose Aadhaar Linked (KYC)
Mainly establishes Aadhaar-bank association for identification & branch compliance. 		DBT Enabled (NPCI Seeded)
Required for Aadhaar-based DBT payment routing from government ministries.
⚡ Payment Readiness Aadhaar Linked (KYC)
Does NOT automatically mean DBT is ready. Direct benefit payments can still fail. 		DBT Enabled (NPCI Seeded)
Indicates DBT/NPCI mapping is in place. Payments sent to Aadhaar arrive smoothly.
🏦 Multiple Bank Accounts Aadhaar Linked (KYC)
Can be linked to MULTIPLE bank accounts across different banks for KYC. 		DBT Enabled (NPCI Seeded)
Can be active in ONLY ONE primary bank account at any given time across all banks in India.
🎓 Student Scholarship Risk Aadhaar Linked (KYC)
⚠️ High risk of PFMS bounce if only KYC linked without explicit NPCI seeding. 		DBT Enabled (NPCI Seeded)
✅ Zero bounce risk. Funds are directly credited via NPCI Aadhaar Payment Bridge.
📝 Action Needed to Activate Aadhaar Linked (KYC)
Submit standard KYC form or submit Aadhaar OTP at bank branch / netbanking. 		DBT Enabled (NPCI Seeded)
Must submit specific 'NPCI Aadhaar Seeding Mandate (Annexure-I)' at bank or via netbanking.
Aadhaar ➔ Bank Account ≠ Aadhaar ➔ NPCI Mapper ➔ Bank Account ➔ DBT

Why does this work flawlessly?

1

The "Only ONE" Rule

You can link Aadhaar to multiple bank accounts for KYC, but only ONE bank account can be active on the NPCI mapper for DBT.

2

The DBT Switch Rule

If you opened a new college bank account, DBT will NOT move automatically. You must submit a mandate to switch seeding to your new account.

3

Offline Check Code

No internet? Dial *99*99*1# from your Aadhaar-registered mobile number anytime to check your active seeded bank.

✅ The 4-Step DBT Resolution & Confirmation Checklist

Step 1 ✓

Submit Mandate at Bank or Online App

Submit the official Annexure-I Mandate Form or perform e-Consent via NetBanking / Postman.

Step 2 ✓

Receive Bank SMS Confirmation

Within 24-48 hours, you will receive an official SMS: 'Aadhaar successfully mapped with NPCI for DBT'.

Step 3 ✓

Verify on NPCI / UIDAI Portal

Check official UIDAI MyAadhaar or NPCI Bharat Aadhaar Seeding Enabler (BASE) portal to verify active green status.

Step 4 ✓

Scholarship Credit Ready!

Your account is now 100% prepared. Scholarship funds released via PFMS will credit straight to your account.
